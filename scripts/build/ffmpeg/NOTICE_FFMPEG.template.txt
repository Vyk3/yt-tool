NOTICE: This application includes ffmpeg and ffprobe, built from FFmpeg
${FFMPEG_VERSION} (LGPL 2.1+) with libmp3lame from LAME ${LAME_VERSION}
(LGPL 2.0).

These binaries are statically linked. Under the LGPL, you may modify
and replace them. To rebuild:

1. Download the source tarballs from the corresponding dependency release
   or directly from:
   - FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-${FFMPEG_VERSION}.tar.xz
   - LAME: https://sourceforge.net/projects/lame/files/lame/${LAME_VERSION}/lame-${LAME_VERSION}.tar.gz

2. Run scripts/build/ffmpeg/build_minimal.sh from the yt-tool repository.

3. Replace the binaries in the app bundle:
   cp ffmpeg  YTTool.app/Contents/Resources/Binaries/ffmpeg
   cp ffprobe YTTool.app/Contents/Resources/Binaries/ffprobe

4. Re-sign the app:
   codesign --force --sign - YTTool.app/Contents/Resources/Binaries/ffmpeg
   codesign --force --sign - YTTool.app/Contents/Resources/Binaries/ffprobe
   codesign --force --deep --sign - YTTool.app

See LICENSE_LGPL.txt and LICENSE_LAME.txt for full license texts.
